Comprehensive Document Checklist for Overseas Employment

This checklist covers essential documents required by candidates applying for overseas blue-collar or technical hands-on positions across the Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain), South Asia, and Europe.

Target roles: Skilled/Semi-skilled
Regions: Middle East • South Asia • Europe
Last Updated: March 2, 2026
✅ Start Early

Begin document collection 3–4 months before travel. Total prep is often 8–16 weeks.

🟨 Copies + Backup

Keep 4–6 photocopies of all docs and scan everything to cloud storage.

🟥 Fraud Warning

Deal only with licensed recruitment agencies and verify offers before payments.

Section 1: Personal Identity Documents

Core identity documents required in almost all destinations.

High Priority
🛂
Valid Passport
⌄
  • Minimum 6 months validity from intended travel date (preferably 2 years remaining)
  • At least two blank visa pages
  • Original required; pages must be clear and undamaged
  • Photocopies: 4–6 copies of passport data page
Original required 4–6 copies 2 blank pages
🪪
National Identity Card
⌄
  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Valid and current
  • Photocopy required (both sides)
👶
Birth Certificate
⌄
  • Original or certified copy
  • Official translation if not in English/Arabic
  • May require attestation for some countries
📸
Passport-Size Photographs
⌄
  • 8–12 recent color photos
  • White or light blue background
  • Biometric specs (usually 35mm × 45mm or per destination)
  • 80% face coverage, clear & professional

Section 2: Educational Certificates

Degrees, diplomas, trade certificates, and attestation sequence.

Attestation-heavy
🎓
Degree/Diploma & Trade Certificates
⌄
  • Original certificates (10th/12th, diploma, ITI)
  • Trade certificates (welding, electrical, HVAC, etc.)
  • Vocational training & apprenticeship completion
📄
Mark Sheets / Transcripts
⌄
  • All semesters/years
  • Original or attested copies
🧰
Technical / Professional Certifications
⌄
  • Skill certificates (welding, electrical, HVAC, etc.)
  • Trade test certificates
  • Safety training (heights, confined spaces, etc.)
  • Equipment licenses (forklift, crane, heavy machinery)
✅
Skill Assessment Certificates
⌄
  • Trade qualification assessments
  • Competency certifications
  • Verification from recognized bodies
Attestation Order (recommended):

Notary → State Education Department → MEA → Embassy (destination) → Chamber of Commerce (if needed).

Section 3: Employment & Experience Documents

Proof of work history, roles, and income.

Career Proof
🧑‍💼
Updated Resume / CV
⌄
  • Employment history with dates & responsibilities
  • Skills/competencies list
  • Contact info (phone, email, address)
  • References included
🏢
Experience Certificates
⌄
  • Letters from all employers (letterhead + stamp)
  • Job title, duties, dates, contact details
  • Often minimum 2 years relevant experience for technical roles
💳
Salary Slips / Pay Stubs
⌄
  • Last 3–6 months salary slips
  • Bank statements showing salary credits
📝
Relieving / Termination Letter
⌄
  • Confirming resignation/completion
  • No dues certificate (if applicable)

Section 4: Legal & Police Clearance

Police clearance and legal status documents.

Mandatory
👮
Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)
⌄
  • From country of origin (mandatory)
  • From countries lived in for > 6 months
  • Recent (typically within 6 months)
  • Embassy attestation may be required
⚖️
Court Clearance (If applicable)
⌄
  • If any legal/criminal history exists
  • Proof of resolution + character certificate
🪖
Military Service Certificate (If applicable)
⌄
  • Discharge / exemption / no-objection as required

Section 5: Medical & Health Documents

Fitness certificate, tests, vaccination records, insurance.

Validity-sensitive
🩺
Medical Fitness Certificate
⌄
  • From government-approved/authorized medical center
  • Validity typically 3–6 months
  • Full medical exam report included
🧪
Medical Tests Required
⌄
  • Blood tests (CBC, blood group, RH)
  • HIV/AIDS, Hepatitis B/C
  • TB test / chest X-ray
  • Malaria (region-specific), drug screening
  • General physical, vision, hearing
💉
Vaccination Records
⌄
  • COVID-19 certificate (WHO-approved vaccines)
  • Yellow fever (if applicable), Polio, others as required
  • International Certificate of Vaccination (Yellow Card)
🛡️
Health Insurance
⌄
  • Proof of coverage (Europe often requires minimum €30,000)
  • Coverage from date of entry; travel insurance for initial period

Section 6: Employment Offer & Contract

Offer letter, signed contract, demand letter, POA.

Employer-issued
📩
Job Offer Letter
⌄
  • Company letterhead + official stamp
  • Job title, salary, benefits, joining date
  • Accommodation/transport details
🧾
Employment Contract
⌄
  • Signed by both parties; terms, salary breakdown, leave
  • Notice/termination clauses; work conditions
  • Translated to worker’s native language (recommended)
📌
Demand Letter / Power of Attorney
⌄
  • Demand letter: positions, categories, quota details
  • POA: if required by agency; notarized & attested

Work visa/permit, forms, sponsorship, receipts.

Match passport
🧷
Work Visa / Permit Approval
⌄
  • Original visa or approval number
  • Validity dates clearly mentioned
🗂️
Visa Application Forms
⌄
  • Completed & signed; accurate information

  • Emergency contact included
🏷️
Sponsorship / Invitation / Receipts
⌄

Section 13: Additional Requirements by Region

Extra documents that vary by destination region.

Region-based

🇪🇺

⌄

  • Trade test certification
  • Biometric work permit card application
  • Singapore MOM approval; security bond (some categories)

Section 15: Important Reminders & Best Practices

Simple habits that prevent delays and rejections.

Do this
  • Start early (3–4 months)
  • Keep 4–6 photocopies of all documents
  • Digital backup: scan & store in cloud
  • Organize in labeled folders
  • Check validity of passport/PCC/medical
  • Verify requirements with employer/agency/embassy
  • Attestation order: Notary → State → MEA → Embassy
  • Use certified translators
  • Keep originals safe during travel
  • Beware of fraud; use licensed agencies only

Section 16: Common Mistakes to Avoid

Most frequent reasons for rework and delay.

Avoid
  • Submitting expired passport/PCC/medical
  • Wrong attestation sequence or missing stamps
  • Photo not matching biometric rules
  • Incomplete employment contract
  • Missing pages in copies
  • Name spelling variations across documents
  • Unauthorized medical centers
  • Forgetting originals during travel
  • Not obtaining emigration clearance (ECR where required)
  • Paying unregistered agents

Section 17: Verification Checklist (48 Hours Before Departure)

Final quick check to avoid airport/immigration issues.

48h
Carry on you:

Passport, visa, contract, medical + PCC, tickets, insurance, employer contacts.

Keep separate bag:

Photocopies + digital copies (phone/email). Originals separate from copies.

  • Passport (min 6 months validity)
  • Work visa stamped / approval
  • Signed employment contract
  • Medical fitness certificate (valid)
  • PCC (recent)
  • Educational + experience certificates (originals + attested copies)
  • Flight tickets confirmed
  • Travel insurance active
  • Accommodation address ready
  • Emergency contacts saved
  • Funds for initial expenses
  • Vaccination certificates
  • Emigration clearance stamp (if ECR)
